Key terms

R-value: how strongly a material resists heat flow; higher resists more. Sellers may measure it differently, so we don't compare or score their figures.
Fit labels: Great fit is 75+ with most of the needed data known; Good fit 60+; Possible 45 to 59; Weak fit below 45.
Data on file: how complete our information on a kit is (Most, Some or Little). It is not a quality rating.

How the fit score works

Each kit is scored 0 to 100 on only what you answered: matching your gear, insulation for your climate, price against your budget, and your priorities. A missing field is not scored as zero. It pulls the score toward 50 and lowers "data on file". Reviews and sellers' R-values are not part of the score yet. The weights are a first draft.

Prices were checked on November 25, 2026 and change often. Confirm at the source. Shipping and tax are not included.
